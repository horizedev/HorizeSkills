# Continuous Idea-to-MVP Governance

## Purpose

Run an evidence-led, founder-directed venture as connected workstreams around one living workbook. Internal research, product definition, design, delivery, review, and revision are continuous. The founder's authorization is required only when a proposed action creates external effects.

## AAS Methods

When the configured AAS stack is available, use `brainstorming`, `idea-refine`, `product-manager`, and `documentation-and-adrs` to frame and coordinate work. Read `workflow/aas-skill-map.yaml` and record the AAS methods used or any catalog gap in the workbook Work Log.

## Required Reading

Before substantive work, read:

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. `workflow/pipeline.yaml`
3. `workflow/IDEA_TO_MVP.md`
4. `aas-stack.json` and `workflow/aas-skill-map.yaml`
5. Current venture's `input/idea-brief.md`
6. Current `artifacts/idea-to-mvp-workbook.md`, evidence, decisions, and relevant app materials

## Continuous Work Procedure

1. Classify new founder input as a commitment, preference, hypothesis, inspiration, or open question.
2. Select the highest-leverage active workstream and matching specialist role.
3. Use the local venture skill and mapped AAS playbooks from `workflow/aas-skill-map.yaml` when available.
4. Update the relevant workbook sections, evidence links, direction log, and work log.
5. Feed new research or delivery constraints into every affected workstream without waiting for a handoff.
6. Validate sources, assumptions, risks, acceptance criteria, and actual test evidence.
7. Request external authorization only when a specific real-world action is proposed.

## Workbook Rules

The living workbook must retain:

- Artifact metadata and status.
- Founder intent and material constraints.
- Evidence sources and limitations.
- Facts, inferences, and assumptions separated.
- Risks, unresolved questions, completion checks, and next work.
- Material direction, scope, and risk changes with rationale.
- An external-authorization log where applicable.

## External-Action Authorization Rules

Explicit founder authorization is required before customer outreach, surveys, messages, publication, ads, pricing publication, purchases, paid subscriptions, domains, paid cloud or API resources, production credentials, DNS, payment settings, production deployment, production-data access, collecting personal data, or public claims of legal, security, compliance, or performance results.

Drafts, local prototypes, research synthesis, design, code, test planning, and non-production work may proceed without an internal approval gate.

## Stop Conditions

Recommend a pause, pivot, smaller test, or stop when:

- No repeated painful problem is evidenced.
- Customers will not commit money, time, data, or behavior change.
- The reachable market cannot support the founder's goals.
- Regulatory, security, or operational risks exceed capacity.
- The MVP cannot be safely built within the founder's stated budget and timeframe.

## Review Checklist

- Does the workbook answer the current venture question rather than describe a generic business?
- Is each strong conclusion proportional to evidence?
- Are negative findings included?
- Are the next test or delivery criteria measurable?
- Does it distinguish what happened from what should happen next?
