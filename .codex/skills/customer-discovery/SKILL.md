# Customer Discovery

## Objective

Establish whether a narrow target segment experiences a frequent, costly, and urgent problem that it already tries to solve.

## Inputs

- Approved `venture_intake_packet`
- Supplied interview notes, call transcripts, support tickets, reviews, community posts, job ads, or workflow artifacts

## Research Sequence

1. Write a falsifiable problem statement.
2. Identify the user, buyer, trigger event, desired outcome, and current workaround.
3. Collect behavioral evidence from recent past events.
4. Compare frequency, urgency, and cost across participants or sources.
5. Identify repeated language and objections.
6. Separate observed pain from a proposed solution.
7. Specify the next cheapest uncertainty-reducing test.

## Interview Questions

Use recent behavior, not hypothetical preference:

- Tell me about the last time this happened.
- What triggered it?
- What did you do step by step?
- Which tools, people, spreadsheets, or workarounds did you use?
- How much time, money, risk, or frustration did it create?
- What failed about the current approach?
- Who owns the budget or decision?
- What would make changing the process urgent?

Avoid: “Would you use this?”, “Do you like this feature?”, and leading descriptions of the proposed solution.

## Evidence Standards

Strong:

- Repeated accounts of the same recent workflow.
- Existing spend, workaround, or behavior change.
- Access to real workflow data.
- A customer asks to be involved or connects a buyer.

Weak:

- Compliments, likes, broad surveys, friends' opinions, or stated intent without behavior.

## Output

Create `problem_validation_packet` with:

- Problem statement and job-to-be-done.
- Target segment and exclusions.
- Evidence table with quotes only from supplied sources.
- Workarounds, pain frequency, costs, triggers, and urgency.
- Interview/recruiting plan if evidence is insufficient.
- Assumptions, risks, limits, and a go/revise/pivot/pause/kill recommendation.
