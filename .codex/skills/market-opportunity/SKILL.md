# Market Opportunity

## Objective

Find a reachable initial market with a meaningful problem, visible alternatives, a credible wedge, and a path to the first customers.

## AAS Methods

When the configured AAS stack is available, apply `competitor-profiling`, `startup-business-analyst-market-opportunity`, and `market-sizing-analysis`. Read `workflow/aas-skill-map.yaml` and record the AAS methods used or any catalog gap in the workbook Work Log.

## Inputs

- Current idea brief, living workbook, and available problem or customer evidence
- Source-backed market and competitor research

## Competitor Method

Map:

1. Direct competitors solving the same job.
2. Indirect competitors solving a related job.
3. Internal tools, spreadsheets, agencies, and manual labor.
4. Non-consumption: users doing nothing.

For each alternative capture:

- Product and source URL.
- Target customer.
- Positioning promise.
- Pricing and date checked.
- Onboarding/setup burden.
- Strengths, weaknesses, and review themes.
- Evidence rather than unsupported opinion.

## Market Sizing Method

Prefer a bottom-up estimate:

```text
reachable target accounts x plausible paid conversion x average monthly revenue = potential MRR
```

State geography, source dates, calculation inputs, uncertainty, and what is excluded. TAM is context; reachable demand and a credible route to the first 100 users matter more at MVP stage.

## Wedge Test

A wedge must be material to the target segment, such as:

- Faster completion of a high-frequency job.
- Simpler setup or a result without configuration.
- Vertical specialization.
- Better trust, privacy, or compliance posture.
- Clear integration into an existing workflow.
- A segment incumbents ignore.

“Better UI” alone is usually insufficient.

## Output

Update the workbook's Market and Alternatives, Demand and Viability, Assumption Map, Risk Register, and Direction Log with a competitor matrix, alternative map, bottom-up sizing, beachhead segment, positioning hypothesis, wedge, channels ranked by reachability, and next demand test.
