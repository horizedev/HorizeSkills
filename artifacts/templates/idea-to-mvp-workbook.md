# Idea-to-MVP Workbook

## How To Use This Workbook

This is the venture's living source of truth. Update the relevant section rather than creating a serial handoff. Preserve earlier founder intent and evidence; record material changes in the direction log.

Use these labels consistently:

- `commitment`: a founder non-negotiable.
- `preference`: a founder direction that may be traded off.
- `hypothesis`: a belief that needs testing.
- `inspiration`: a useful reference or starting point.
- `open_question`: an unresolved choice or unknown.
- `fact`, `inference`, `assumption`: claim confidence labels.
